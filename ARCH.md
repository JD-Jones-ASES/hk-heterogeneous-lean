# Architecture and lane protocol (working document; not part of the published repository)

## What this repository proves

Mirtabatabaei–Bullo (MB; SIAM J. Control Optim. 50 (2012) 2763–2785, arXiv:1103.2829v2) study `n`
agents with opinions `x ∈ ℝⁿ` and bounds `r ∈ ℝⁿ_{>0}` updating by `x(t+1) = A(x(t)) x(t)` (their (2.1)),
row `i` of `A(y)` the uniform average over `N_i(y)`; SBC: `N_i(y) = {j : |y_i − y_j| ≤ r_i}`; SBI:
`{j : |y_i − y_j| ≤ r_j}`. Conjecture 2.2: `G_r(x(t))` is eventually constant. Conjecture 2.3: every
trajectory freezes or is eventually pseudo-stable ((2.2): converging; two non-empty classes, fixed at
the limit or strictly monotone on one side of it). Theorem 6.4(iv): an eventually constant digraph
gives eventual pseudo-stability. Hegarty–Ognissanti–Wedin (HOW; arXiv:2610.03229) refute all three
with four systems whose trajectories are `x_∞ + λᵗ v`. We pin MB's definitions verbatim
(`Challenge.lean`, 36 definitions) and prove 35 statements: the closed forms and the full
neighbourhood tables of the four systems, convergence, non-freezing, non-pseudo-stability, the nine
negations plus the literal one, `fvct` and the per-step factor along the two constant-digraph systems
(`= λ < 0`, converging to no `ρ ≥ 0`), MB's Lemma 4.2 and Lemma 4.8 (the positive side), and the
sharpness (`ε = 0` at the alternating agents of the two alternating systems).

Agents are 0-indexed in Lean: the paper's agent `i` is index `i − 1`.

## The mathematics, module by module

See the section *Proof plans* below (the generic lane's report, verbatim) and the module docstrings.

**The step and the tables (SBC7, SBI7, SBC6, SBI7b).** `step_apply` turns `step` into the mean over
the neighbourhood. With `u = λᵗ` an atom, every membership inequality `|x_i − x_j| ≤ R` is affine in
`u`, so each table is decided on a `u`-interval by `linarith` from the endpoint bounds: `0 < u ≤ 1`
(even `t`) and `−1/6 ≤ u < 0` (odd `t`, SBC) or `λ ≤ u < 0` (odd `t`, SBI; `−1 ≤ u` is NOT enough for
SBI7, whose odd table changes near `u ≈ −0.76`). Then the update identity: the mean of `x_∞` over each
row's table is `x_{∞,i}` and the mean of `v` over it is `λ v_i`; for SBI through `φ² = φ + 1`,
`4λ = 1 − φ`, `4λφ = −1` (`Basic`). Induction on `t` with `traj_succ`, `pow_succ`, the sign of `λᵗ`
by `Nat.even_or_odd` (`Even.neg_pow`/`Odd.neg_pow` for `−1/6`; for `λ = ψ/4 ∈ (−1/4, 0)` use
`Even.pow_pos`, `Odd.pow_neg`, and `pow_le_one₀`/`one_le_pow` style bounds), closes the closed form,
and the table theorem follows from the closed form and the per-parity table lemma.

**Non-freezing and non-pseudo-stability (Basic).** An agent `i` with offset `λᵗ v_i`, `λ < 0`,
`v_i ≠ 0`, is never at the same value twice in a row, so `FixedFrom` fails at every `τ`; and in
`PseudoStableAfter x xinf τ` the limit `xinf` equals `x_∞` (`tendsto_nhds_unique`), so `i ∈ F`
contradicts the nonzero offset at `τ`, and `i ∈ C` needs `x τ i` and `x (τ+1) i` strictly on the same
side of `x_{∞,i}`, contradicting `λ^(τ+1) v_i = λ · λ^τ v_i` having the opposite sign.

**fvct and the per-step factor (Basic, SBC6, SBI7b).** When the neighbourhoods along the trajectory are
constant from `t` on, `(A(x t))^s.mulVec (x t) = x (t+s)` by induction (`pow_succ'`, `Matrix.mulVec_mulVec`,
`traj_succ`), so `fvct (x t)` is the limit (`Filter.Tendsto.limUnder_eq` with
`Filter.tendsto_add_atTop_iff_nat`). The factor `(x(t+1)_i − L_i)/(x(t)_i − L_i) = λ` by `div_eq_iff`.

**Lemma 4.2 and Lemma 4.8 (Generic).** `2 ε_i(z) ≤ ||z_i − z_j| − R|` for `j ≠ i`, both `R`
(`csInf_le`, bounded below by `0`). For `j ≠ i`: if `j ∈ N_i(z)` then `|y_i − y_j| ≤ |z_i − z_j| +
ε_i + ε_j ≤ R`; if not, `|y_i − y_j| ≥ |z_i − z_j| − ε_i − ε_j ≥ R` with strictness from the
`ε > 0` case or equality `y = z` from the `ε = 0` case (`abs_sub_abs_le_abs_sub`, `abs_le`, `abs_lt`,
`linarith` per case). For `j = i` both sides read `0 ≤ R` (no positivity of `r` is needed). Lemma 4.8(i):
`tendsto_pi_nhds` + `Metric.tendsto_atTop` give, for each `i`, a tail with `|x t i − x_{∞,i}| < ε_i`;
`Filter.eventually_all` over `Fin n`; then Lemma 4.2. (ii): the digraphs equal give the neighbourhoods
equal (`neighbors_eq_of_proximityDigraph_eq`), so `fvct_eq_of_constant_neighbors`; the equilibrium by
continuity of `A.mulVec` coordinatewise (`tendsto_finset_sum`, `Tendsto.const_mul`) and uniqueness of
limits.

**Sharpness (Refute).** `csInf S = 0` by `le_antisymm (csInf_le ⟨0, …⟩ ⟨j, hj, …⟩) (le_csInf ⟨_, _⟩ …)`
with the witness `j` at distance exactly `R` (sbc7: index 2 or 4 against index 3 with `R = r 3 = 12`;
sbi7: index 3 against index 1 with `R = r 1 = 4`, and symmetrically index 5).

## Rules (binding for every lane)

1. **Pinned statements.** Every statement in a skeleton file keeps its exact name, binders and
   conclusion. Add helper lemmas and definitions freely in your own file. If a pinned statement is
   wrong or unprovable as stated, do not edit it: prove the nearest correct version under a new name,
   leave the pinned one with its `sorry`, and say so first in your report.
2. **Other lanes' files are black boxes.** Use their sorried statements as stated; never edit them,
   never prove them yourself. If you need a variant, prove an `aux_…` copy in your file and report it.
3. **Build only through** `bash scripts/lane-build.sh HK.<Module>` from your worktree root, one
   module per call (two machine-wide slots; waits for 2.5 GB free). Never `lake build` with no
   target, never `lake update`, never `lake exe cache get`. Scratch files go in `.scratch/` of your
   worktree (`lake env lean .scratch/<file>.lean` only when a slot is free). Nothing detached, no
   background jobs. Never run the core-notation audit (it takes 4 GB).
4. **Kernel-only.** No `sorry`, `admit`, `axiom`, `native_decide`, `unsafe`, `partial`,
   `implemented_by`, `extern`, `ofReduceBool`, `debug.skipKernelTC`, `debug.byAsSorry` in the
   deliverable. If you raise `maxHeartbeats`, do it per declaration and report it. Keep the `module`
   header, `public import`s, `@[expose] public section … end HK`.
5. **Imports.** `Defs.lean` imports `Mathlib`, as the Challenge does; the development modules may keep
   `public import HK.Basic` etc. (they inherit Mathlib). Do not add imports of other lanes' modules.
6. **Lean 4.35.0-rc2 gotchas:** `le_or_gt` not `le_or_lt`; `push Not` not `push_neg`;
   `Finset.notMem_empty`; `pow_le_one₀`; `Nat.even_or_odd`; a section `variable` hypothesis is dropped
   from a theorem whose statement does not mention it (`include`); `Finset` literals of `Fin` numerals
   are `insert` chains (`decide` settles membership; `Finset.ext` + `fin_cases` + `simp` for equalities);
   `![…]` evaluates by `Matrix.cons_val_zero`/`Matrix.cons_val_succ` or `simp [Matrix.vecCons]`;
   real numerals by `norm_num`; `dotProduct` (not `Matrix.dotProduct`) at this pin.
7. **No names** of people or AI systems in the Lean; no "record" language anywhere.
8. **Done means:** your module builds through `lane-build.sh` with zero `sorry` warnings in your
   file (other lanes' sorries show as warnings on replayed modules, which is fine); `git add` your
   file and `git commit -m "<one line>"` on your branch (never push, never checkout `main`); return the
   structured report (every pinned statement closed or open with the reason, helpers, `aux_`
   duplicates, heartbeats, the commit hash, the tail of the last build, anything the desk must know).
